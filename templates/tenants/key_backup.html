Where to put it

Good places:

  • A password manager your church already uses — as a secure note.
  • Printed and kept in a locked cabinet or safe with your other sensitive records.
  • On an encrypted USB drive stored off-site.

Not these:

  • An email to yourself, or any shared mailbox.
  • A shared drive, a chat message, or a document anyone at the church can open.
  • A sticky note on the monitor.

Think of it the way you would a master password. Anyone with both this key and a copy of the database could read your volunteers' personal information — which is exactly why the information is encrypted in the first place.
